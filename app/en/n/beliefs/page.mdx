# Things I Believe

[Douglas Pereira](/en)

## Shipping is a skill

- Done and in front of people beats perfect in a branch
- Small teams move because they have to
- Write the first version, then keep the loop going

## Writing is part of the job

- If I can't explain it, I don't understand it yet
- Notes are for future me, not for a blog calendar
- Teaching something is how it actually sticks

## Tools should disappear

- The best developer experience is the one you stop noticing
- Complexity has to earn its place
- Personal software is allowed to be weird and small

## Lead by doing

- Titles are optional; showing the work is not
- Hire for taste and curiosity, then get out of the way
- Be the person who writes it down when nobody else will
