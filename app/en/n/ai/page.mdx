# Understanding AI

[Douglas Pereira](/en)

## It is a tool, not a teammate

- It does not know your product
- It does not feel the deadline
- You still own the diff

## Use it for the middle

- First drafts, not final calls
- Boilerplate, not architecture
- Exploration, then you decide

## The skill is the question

- Bad prompts are vague tickets
- Show the constraint, the file, the failure
- If you cannot describe the outcome, you are not ready to generate it

## Keep taste

- Fast output is not good output
- Read everything before you merge
- The model will happily invent an API that does not exist
