# Shipping

[Douglas Pereira](/en)

## Land it

- A branch is not a product
- Behind a flag still counts
- Behind a meeting does not

## Shrink the first version

- If it needs a launch, it is probably too big
- Cut scope until you are slightly embarrassed
- Then ship, and listen

## The loop is the strategy

- Build, show, change your mind
- Status updates are not feedback
- Customers will tell you the real ticket

## Momentum compounds

- Teams that ship learn faster
- Teams that wait get better at waiting
- Make the next ship smaller than the last excuse
