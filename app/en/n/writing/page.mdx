# Clear writing

[Douglas Pereira](/en)

## Writing is thinking

- The fuzzy idea dies on the page
- If the paragraph is long, the thought is not done
- Delete the throat-clearing

## Write for the person who was not in the room

- Names, not pronouns
- Decision, then context
- Links to the thing, not "as discussed"

## Notes beat memory

- Slack is not an archive
- Future you is a stranger
- One short doc prevents ten meetings

## Tone can be human

- Direct is not rude
- Jargon is not intelligence
- Say the quiet risk out loud
