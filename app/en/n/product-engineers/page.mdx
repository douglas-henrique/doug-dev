# Product engineers

[Douglas Pereira](/en)

## Own the outcome

- The ticket is a guess
- Talk to the person who feels the pain
- Measure something, even if it is crude

## Design is not a handoff

- Sit with the blank state
- Copy is part of the interface
- If it is awkward to use, it is not done

## Full stack is a direction

- Enough backend to ship the feature
- Enough frontend to not hide behind "that's design"
- Ask for help without throwing the problem over the wall

## Taste is the differentiator

- Lots of people can make it work
- Fewer people can make it obvious
- That is the job now
