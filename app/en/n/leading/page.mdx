# Leading engineers

[Douglas Pereira](/en)

## Still do the work

- You cannot review what you no longer understand
- A lead who only schedules becomes a bottleneck of meetings
- Keep a small surface area of real code

## Clarity is the gift

- Ambiguous goals create busy people
- Write the decision, then invite disagreement
- Repeat yourself more than feels dignified

## Hire for slope

- Current skill is a snapshot
- Curiosity and taste travel
- Hell yes or no

## Make others louder

- Give away the interesting work
- Credit in public, critique in private
- Your scoreboard is what the team can ship without you
